Install in three steps

Works on the web version (tested on 2.058). Keep the game in its own visible window: browsers slow down timers in background tabs.

Open the game

Load Cookie Clicker and wait for your save to appear.

Load the script

Drag this to your bookmarks bar and click it on the game page:

🍪 CCAuto

…or copy the script and paste it into the console (F12).

Lean back

A small CCAuto window shows what it's doing. Drag it by its title bar wherever it's out of the way, and fold it with –. Stop everything with CCAuto.stop().

What it does

One coordinator instead of a pile of timers, so nothing fights over your bank.

👆

10 clicks per second

Time-based clicking, so the rate stays even when timers jitter. Every golden cookie, wrath cookie and reindeer is popped.

🔮

Spells it can predict

Force the Hand of Fate is deterministic per save. The script replays the game's RNG, casts only into combos, skips bad outcomes with cheap spells and double casts by selling Wizard towers.

💥

Godzamok stacking

During Click frenzy it sells and rebuys cheap buildings in rounds. Each round adds +1% click power per building, ×301 in testing.

🏗️

Smart shopping

Buildings and upgrades are ranked by payback time, using the CpS each one would really add (synergies, kittens and clicks included). A reserve stays in the bank for Lucky! and combos.

🐉

Krumblor & Pantheon

Trains the dragon, sets the simulated-best auras once, and slots Godzamok, Mokalsium and Muridal.

🌱

Garden research

Breeds all 34 seeds with proper layouts, plants only when seeds are cheap, then fills the plot with Whiskerbloom.

📈

Stock market

Thresholds per good, fitted on a port of the game's price model: about 129 $/h for every unit of stock held across all 18 goods, checked on a separate price run.

🎃

Seasons

Collects Valentine's, Christmas, Easter and Halloween drops, then settles in the season with the best permanent boost.

🐛

Gentle with wrinklers

Wrinklers keep eating (and multiplying) your cookies. They're popped only for season drops, never the shiny ones.

Good to know

Dragon Orbs and Godzamok don't mix. Every sale grants Godzamok's buff before the game checks Orbs' "no buffs" rule, so the wish never happens. The script warns you if you configure both.
Double casting needs about 600+ Wizard towers at tower level 10. With fewer, the script waits instead of wasting magic.
Elder stage 1 can't be reached once you own Communal brainsweep or Elder Pact. In that case set CCAuto.cfg.ELDER_STAGE = 3.
Tune it to your save: run copy(JSON.stringify(CCAuto.snapshot())), save it as snapshot.json, then uv run strategy_sim/run_strategies.py snapshot.json.
